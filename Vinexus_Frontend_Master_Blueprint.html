1. Project Overview & Business Domain

Vinexus is an online Lead Generation & Product Catalog Platform for a leading CCTV and Security Equipment Dealer in India.

Core Product Catalog:

  • CCTV Cameras (e.g. TrueView, CP Plus, Hikvision)
  • DVRs / NVRs & Storage Units
  • Modems & Industrial Routers
  • LAN Wires & Networking Cables
  • Security Accessories & Hardware Supplies

Key Rule: Vinexus is NOT a transactional e-commerce store. There are NO online payments or checkout steps. From the cart, users either click "Chat on WhatsApp" or "Send Enquiry".

3. Form Validation & File Upload Rules

  • Login Identifier: Valid Email or 10-digit Indian Mobile (^[6-9]\d{9}$).
  • OTP Code: Exactly 6 numeric digits (^\d{6}$).
  • GSTIN: 15-character uppercase string (^[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}[1-9A-Z]{1}Z[0-9A-Z]{1}$).
  • PAN: 10-character uppercase string (^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}$).
  • Aadhar: 12-digit numeric string (^\d{12}$).
  • File Uploads: Max 5 MB per file. Accepted types: .jpg, .png, .webp, .pdf.
